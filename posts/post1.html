Understanding Bayesian Inference in Practice

Published on October 10, 2025

Bayesian inference offers a coherent framework for reasoning under uncertainty. It provides a systematic way to update our beliefs as new data become available. In contrast to classical methods, Bayesian analysis incorporates prior knowledge through a prior distribution, which is then updated using observed data to form the posterior distribution.
